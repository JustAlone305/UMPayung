[UM CAMPUS EMERGENCY]
Source authority: Universiti Malaya Registrar, Security Info
Source URL: https://registrar.um.edu.my/news/security-info
- For an emergency on the UM campus, contact the UM Security Office emergency hotline at 03-7967 7777.
- Follow directions issued by authorized campus emergency personnel.
- The public UM OSHEC page describes campus emergency management and directs staff/students to UMPORTAL for detailed procedures. Do not claim access to unpublished UM protocols.
UM OSHEC source: https://oshe.um.edu.my/prosedur-kesiagaan-dan-tindakan-kecemasan

[FLOOD SAFETY]
Source authority: Malaysian Meteorological Department (MET Malaysia)
Source URL: https://www.met.gov.my/en/pendidikan/fenomena-cuaca/
- Stay away from fast-flowing drains, rivers, and flooded areas.
- Do not drive into floodwater or cross unsafe bridges and flood-prone roads.
- Follow instructions from local disaster-management authorities.
- Move to higher ground when flash flooding is expected and it is safe to do so.
- Park vehicles in higher locations before flooding where safe and feasible; never enter floodwater to retrieve a vehicle.

[THUNDERSTORM SAFETY]
Source authority: Malaysian Meteorological Department (MET Malaysia)
Source URL: https://www.met.gov.my/en/pendidikan/fenomena-cuaca/
- Check weather warnings and postpone outdoor activities when thunderstorms are expected.
- During thunderstorms, seek shelter inside a sturdy building or enclosed vehicle.
- Do not shelter under an isolated tree; stay away from water.
- Be alert to strong winds, falling branches, and flash floods.

[HAZE AND AIR QUALITY]
Source authority: Malaysia Department of Environment (DOE) APIMS; Ministry of Health Malaysia (MOH)
Source URLs:
https://eqms.doe.gov.my/APIMS/main
https://www.moh.gov.my/en/core/health-information/jerebu/artikel-dan-panduan-pengguna-berkaitan-jerebu/langkah-langkah-pencegahan-semasa-jerebu
- API 0-50: Good; 51-100: Moderate; 101-200: Unhealthy; 201-300: Very Unhealthy; above 300: Hazardous.
- When air quality is unhealthy, reduce strenuous outdoor activities and stay indoors where feasible.
- Those with heart or respiratory conditions and other high-risk individuals should take extra care and follow medical advice.
- If outside exposure is unavoidable, use suitable respiratory protection as advised by health authorities.
- Seek medical assessment if breathing problems or other concerning symptoms occur.
- Use the official DOE APIMS source for current API readings, not a static value in this document.

[CHATBOT BOUNDARIES - PROJECT DESIGN, NOT AN OFFICIAL UM POLICY]
- Answer questions related to campus storms, flooding, falling trees, blocked drains, haze, and immediate environmental safety.
- Distinguish official published guidance from project recommendations and from unverified information.
- Never claim real-time conditions, open roads, water depth, safe routes, or official UM instructions without current verified information.
- For immediate campus emergencies, advise contacting UM Security at 03-7967 7777; if in danger, seek local emergency assistance.
- If a question is unrelated to environmental or campus safety, politely redirect to the app's safety scope.
- Do not treat AI photo-based severity estimates as verified hazard status.
